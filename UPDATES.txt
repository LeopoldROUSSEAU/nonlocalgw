Clearly, the nonlocal runs generate lower MSE Loss. Also, having the four features leads to lesser error that the having the three features.
Since less noise in the stratosphere and weaker flux magnitudes, the MSE Loss is weaker for the stratosphere only runs, than for the global runs.
Attention UNet provides the lower error for the three features input. However, for four features, the errors from Attention UNet are not the lowest. For four, they are definitey much lower than 1x1 but might be slightly higher than 3x3 or 5x5. No clear patter for u,v,theta,w.
